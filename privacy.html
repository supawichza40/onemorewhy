Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

1. Overview

One More Why Studio uses a private tool to publish its own videos and photos to its own TikTok account. This policy explains what data that tool handles.

2. Data we access

  • Basic TikTok profile information of the studio's own account (such as display name, avatar and account ID), received through TikTok Login Kit.
  • Content we upload: the studio's own videos and photos and their captions, sent to TikTok through the Content Posting API.
  • Access tokens that let the tool act on the studio's own account. These are stored securely with our integration provider, Composio.

We do not collect data from visitors to this website, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

3. How we use data

Only to sign in to the studio's own TikTok account and publish the studio's own content. We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Retention and deletion

Access tokens are kept only while the connection is active. You can remove access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services. To ask us to delete any data, email onemorewhystudio2026@gmail.com.

5. Third parties

Data goes only to TikTok, as needed to publish content, and to Composio, which securely stores the connection. Their privacy policies apply to how they handle it.

6. Changes

We will update the date above if this policy changes.
